Sensors

0–300 psi · 0.5–4.5 V

Rear manifold, shared by both tanks. Read by the STM32 (A0) and the safety node, which trips everything above 210 psi.

0–300 psi · 0.5–4.5 V

Port block behind the top chamber (A1). In valve A meters that chamber against it, so it sets the top cannon's shot pressure.

0–300 psi · 0.5–4.5 V

Port block behind the bottom chamber (A2). In valve B meters against it, so each cannon has its own pressure.

battery · PDH bus

Two dividers (A4, A5). Battery level for the dashboard; the PDH reading confirms the contactor closed and catches an E-stop or a welded contact.

capacitive switch · ~20%

XKC-Y25 taped to the outside of the printed tank. Pump run time gives the % gauge; the switch re-syncs it and stops the pump about a minute after it reads dry.

core 220–240 °C · box < 70 °C

MAX6675 thermocouple on the fog core (SPI). Three DS18B20s on one pin: fogger box (heater off at 70 °C), electronics shelf, battery bay.
