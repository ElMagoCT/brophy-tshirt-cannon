# Waterjet parts (frame brackets)

Every bracket on the box-tube frame, flat, in millimetres, from `cad/glacis/brackets.py` (the CAD's own numbers, regenerated by every `robot.py` run). Nothing is bent. Each DXF is one part (layer CUT: outline, pockets and holes); `nest_*` are the parts laid out on 12 x 12 in sheets (layer SHEET is the sheet edge).

- Holes of 4.9 mm are for 3/16 blind rivets (a #11 drill size): the matching holes in the tubes are in the CAD's tube parts, so the CNC program for each extrusion comes from the same model.
- Parts marked CSK take 3/16 countersunk rivets: countersink those holes 120 deg (the rivets on the parts list are 120 deg heads) on the outer face after cutting (the side plates sit right beside the wheels; the bottom corner gussets and the bellypan face the underglow and the floor).
- 1/8 in = 3.175 mm, 1/16 in = 1.59 mm, 6061 or 5052.
