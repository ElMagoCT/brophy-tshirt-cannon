# Waterjet parts (frame plates, 40x40 T-slot frame)

Every flat plate on the 40x40 T-slot frame (branch extrusion-4040), in millimetres, from `cad/glacis/brackets.py` (the CAD's own numbers, regenerated by every `robot.py` run). Nothing is bent. Each DXF is one part (layer CUT: outline, pockets and holes); `nest_*` are the parts laid out on 12 x 12 in sheets (layer SHEET is the sheet edge).

- Holes of 5.5 mm are for M5 screws into drop-in / roll-in T-nuts in the extrusion's 6 mm slots: no holes in the extrusion for them, so the plates go on anywhere along a slot. The shop list for the extrusion itself (lengths, end taps, 10 mm access holes) is `docs/glacis/cut_list_4040.csv`.
- Parts marked CSK take M5 countersunk screws: countersink those holes 90 deg to 10.4 mm on the outer face after cutting (the bearing plates sit right beside the wheels; the bottom corner plates and the bellypan face the underglow and the floor). The rest take M5 x 10 button heads.
- The bearing plates (3 per wheel: the inner one on the side plate, two stacked on the outer rail) take the flanged 1/2 hex bearing as a press fit (28.68 mm): ream to fit the bearing in hand.
- 1/8 in = 3.175 mm, 6061 or 5052.
